Notes

  1. All interior walls are non-bearing partitions under a clear-span 9'-0" flat ceiling. Headers: a single flat 2x4 for every opening up to 8' (IRC R602.7.4), cripples at 16" to the double top plate. Pocket doors use the frame maker's header.
  2. Ceiling: roof trusses run east-west (along Y) at 24" o.c. Partitions that run east-west sit between trusses and need 2x6 flat blocking at 24" between the bottom chords to catch the top plate; partitions that run north-south cross a truss every 24". Hang every top plate with slip clips (1" gap, no nails through the plate into the chord) to avoid truss uplift cracking.
  3. Walls are drawn at 5" (2x4 + 1/2" board each side). A 2x6 wet wall is 6-1/2"; the extra 1-1/2" grows toward the side named in the schedule, so the room on the other side keeps its drawn dimension.
  4. Doors: 8'-0" leaves. Swing door RO = leaf + 2" wide × 98-1/2" tall. Pocket door RO = 2 × leaf + 1" wide × 100-1/2" tall; the dashed box shows the pocket side. D3 is a 42" cased opening. D4, D5 (36" pockets) and D8, D9 each have a partition abutting the pocket side of the wall (walls 15, 11 and 12): the abutting wall nails to the pocket frame's reinforced split studs, not through the cavity.
  5. Staggered-stud walls (green): 2x6 plates, 2x4 studs alternating faces at 12" o.c. (24" o.c. on each face), R-19 batt woven between, no stud touches both faces. Pipes and the washer box thread between the studs; the dryer gets a recessed dryer box in wall 17. 2x10 wall 23 (purple): 2x10 plates and studs at 16" o.c.; the pocket frame for D18 sits in the bedroom half, the vanity drains and the WC vent in the bath half.
  6. Exterior furring (grey dashed): 2x4 @ 24" o.c. in the 7" band, standing 1/2" off the brick as an air gap. Shown in the 3D model; nothing changes there except the blocking listed.
  7. Every door has at least 32" clear and 18" latch-side clearance on the pull side per the plan; keep thresholds at 1/2" max (1/4" at the shower, which has none).
  8. Curbless shower (X 2'-6" to 8'-0", Y 32' to 37'): recess the floor 2" over the pad and 12" beyond its open side, slope 1/4" per foot to the linear drain on the bench side.
  9. Blocking is shown as magenta bands on the face noted. Grab-bar blocking is continuous 2x8 flat with its centre at 34" AFF; vanity blocking 2x10 flat 26"–34" AFF (wall-hung roll-under vanities, 27" clear beneath); TV blocking 3/4" plywood 42"–66" AFF.
  10. Sound: R-13 batts in every bedroom and bathroom wall and in walls 7, 9, 17, 19, 20 and 23.
  11. Exterior walls: brick shell with the measured 7" furring. New blocking in the furring is shown on the sheet; nothing structural changes there. No water piping in the furring (see P-1).
  12. Elevator hoistway walls (2, 3, 4) and the stair opening are as measured; the stair guard and handrails follow IRC R311.7.8 and R312 (34"–38" rail, 36" guard, 4" sphere).
  13. Sub-panel P2 sits on the landing face of wall 1 so it stays reachable.
